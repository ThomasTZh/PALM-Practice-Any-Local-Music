# M3.5 plan — Decode beat and downbeat candidates

Spec: `projectPlans/milestone3.md` §7 (M3.5). Fifth Milestone 3 work
package. Consumes published activation artifacts without torch and
produces source-aligned accepted/candidate pulses with a versioned
decoder profile. No automatic result publication, tempo map, or meter.

**Acceptance (§7):** decoded pulse objects are source-aligned, versioned
separately from raw activations, and preserve ambiguous evidence for
later correction.

## Scope in / out

In:
- `domain/rhythm_decode.py`: `BeatDecoderProfile`,
  `BEAT_THIS_DECODER_PROFILE`, `SourceFrameFraction`, `DecodedPulse`,
  `DecodedRhythmResult` with source-alignment validation.
- `rhythm/decoder.py`: torch-free reimplementation of the pinned minimal
  postprocessor (7-frame local maxima, positive-logit acceptance,
  adjacent-peak dedup, downbeat snapping), source-frame mapping and
  half-up rounding, collision handling, candidate retention,
  plausibility flags, and deterministic decoded content hash.
- Tests with synthetic activations plus real activation decoding in the
  skipped-by-default integration test.
- Evidence.

Out:
- Automatic `RhythmResult`/branch publication and project persistence
  (M3.6).
- Tempo map, pulse-unit interpretation, meter inference (M3.7–M3.9).
- Decoder API/UI and durable jobs (M3.17).
- Decoder cache identity (M3.18); the decoded hash already covers the
  decoder profile and activation identity.
- GPU-specific decoder behavior (decoding is deterministic CPU code).

## Decisions

1. **Default decoder = pinned upstream minimal postprocessing.** The
   profile is explicit and versioned; changing parameters creates a new
   decoded identity without touching activations.
2. **Raw scores are uncalibrated.** Confidence kind is `heuristic`,
   `calibrated=false`, value = sigmoid(logit); the result never presents
   these as probabilities of correctness.
3. **Two layers.** Accepted pulses seed tempo-map construction;
   candidates retain low-confidence peaks, endpoint failures, and
   collision losers. Accepted source frames are unique by construction.
4. **Source rounding.** Model frames are processed as exact fractions
   (dedup means and downbeat snapping can be fractional); the source
   frame fraction is stored and rounded once, half-up, at the final
   boundary. Endpoint tolerance is 1 frame, recorded as
   `clamped_endpoint`; materially outside events become candidates.
5. **Downbeats are pulses.** A downbeat that snaps onto a beat is merged
   into one accepted pulse carrying both logits. Existence of downbeats
   never implies a meter.
6. **Flags are evidence, not destruction.** Tiny intervals, long gaps,
   tempo instability, weak downbeats, and downbeats-without-beats are
   recorded; no pulse is deleted because of a flag.
7. **Content hash** is deterministic over activation identity, profile,
   project geometry, aggregated pulse evidence, and flags. Random IDs are
   excluded so reruns agree.
