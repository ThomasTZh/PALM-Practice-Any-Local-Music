# M3.14 plan — Synchronized metronome (in progress)

Spec: `projectPlans/milestone3.md` §16 (M3.14).

## Completed core

- Click schedule domain/strength/subdivision.
- Grid-derived clicks with compound/irregular grouping accents.
- Deterministic source→render mapping and loop-repetition math without
  cumulative rounding drift.
- Metronome schedule API using persisted settings; Python and TS tests.

- Procedural worklet click tables/mixer after the stretcher, bounded
  absolute-frame schedules, transport API, and a playing lookahead
  scheduler in the playback panel.

## Remaining

- Loop-wrap-aware scheduling, seek/rate invalidation of committed
  near-term clicks, and the transport generation ledger integration.
- Audible acceptance at variable tempo/speed and loop wraps.
- Uncertain/unmetered click policy UI.

Core complete, package in progress.
